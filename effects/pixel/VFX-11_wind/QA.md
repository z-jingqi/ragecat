# Whirlwind validation

- 2022 component assertions: gradual entry, sustain, graceful stop, natural end, 30/60/144 Hz, 1000 reuse cycles, parameter caps, invalid-number/color fallback, disabled leaves, opposite rotation and repeatable analytic geometry.
- 8 demo assertions: pause, single-step, reverse and low-vortex presets, live radius, stop and reset.
- Native Godot interaction reviewed at actual 1× and enlarged 2×: pause/replay/frame-step, stop-to-empty, reset, radius and light background.
- Fresh 12.0167-second 60 Hz viewport recording, 721 frames. Every frame's 2× panel is exactly the nearest expansion of 1×.
- Four presets reviewed: jade, counter-clockwise, low ground vortex and dust on light background. Geometry stays inside canvas and visibly rotates throughout sustain. Enter/exit use shaped geometry rather than whole-image pop.
- Production code and textures are procedural; the reference poster/strip was not included as a game asset.

These are prototype self-checks, not a claim of Unity integration or final art approval.

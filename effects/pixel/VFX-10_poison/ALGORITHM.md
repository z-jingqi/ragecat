# Construction

A stable footprint anchors the visual. Its surface curls drift gently. Gas slots use seed-derived positions and phase offsets; each rises, expands, changes from green to shaded green, then geometrically contracts to zero before its phase wraps. Sorted vertical centers give the low rolling masses depth. Each lobe has a stepped ellipse, small crest and negative-space crease instead of a uniform round disk. Five bubbles separately grow, rise, then split briefly into three flecks.

All motion is evaluated from absolute age. No random draw occurs in the render loop, no simulation emitter list grows, and render frequency does not change the state. Formation and exit are smooth spatial envelopes, applied to both footprint and gas. Gameplay duration is independent of exit.

This is a stylized 2D self-drawn gas loop, not a fluid simulation and not a reproduction of the HD reference. It is engine-neutral in math but current implementation is Godot only.

# Visual research

- https://particlefx.studio/effects/electric-net-trap — actual eight-moment strip inspected. Reference for short repeated currents and sparse knot sparks, not its dense uniform web or rotating concentric structure. Its sprites, engine data and source are not copied.
- https://particlefx.studio/effects/pixel-art-lightning-strike — actual strip inspected; coordinated palette and irregular layered geometry with this library's revised strike. No source dependency on the single-strike scene.
- https://mimicmart.itch.io/pixel-art-lightning-spell-effects — inspected downloaded storm/strike GIF contact sheet shared by the area-burst research pass. Distinct short columns and sparse local impacts reinforce separation and negative space rather than continuous uniform noise. No assets copied or license assumed.
- https://crunchyvfx.com/ — reviewed generator capabilities and license text. Its exported effects are stated CC0; that does not cover the application source or imported artwork. No exported assets or tool source copied.
- https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/2d/custom_drawing_in_2d.html — official custom drawing and pixel-line alignment guidance.

All delivered drawing, timing and geometry code is independently authored. Downloaded research images stay outside the project and are not production assets.

## Temporal revision reference study
Re-inspected the actual ParticleFX lightning 8-moment strip: initial connected bright bolt becomes sparse cool remnants while local ground sparks persist. Its documented staggered emitters support delayed secondary motion. Adapted that timing principle into path growth, delayed forks and independently shrinking cooled segments; no borrowed textures, screen flash or smoke layer.

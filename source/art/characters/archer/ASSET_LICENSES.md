# Archer asset provenance

This notice covers the third-party source data used by the Archer artwork. It does not change the license of the game or other project files.

## MakeHuman Community — CC0

The hm08 body mesh and Asian young-adult male target contain explicit September 2020 CC0 declarations. Their listed rights holders are Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, and Jonas Hauquier.

- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/master/makehuman/data/targets/macrodetails/asian-male-young.target

The young Asian male skin, eyes/brown eye material, and ponytail01 hair data come from the official CC0 system asset pack, with matching per-asset CC0 declarations.

- https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
- https://files2.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip

## Rehman Polanski — CC0

The fitted trousers and boots derive from the Viking pants and boots in the official suits02 pack. Each source .mhclo identifies Rehman Polanski as author and CC0 as its license.

- https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/suits02.html
- https://files2.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/suits02/suits02_cc0.zip

## Kay Lousberg — CC0

KayKit Character Animations 1.1 was used as a motion-timing reference. The pack's License.txt explicitly permits personal and commercial use under CC0. The Archer uses a separately fitted rig, constrained hand/bow poses, and project-authored motion data; no restricted third-party animation source is distributed.

- https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-character-animations
- https://kaylousberg.com/

## Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven — CC0

The quiver's leather material uses Brown Leather by Rob Tuytel. The source files are brown_leather_albedo_4k.jpg, brown_leather_nor_gl_4k.jpg and brown_leather_rough_4k.jpg. The official asset page identifies the author and CC0 license. Poly Haven permits commercial use and redistribution of these asset files.

- https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_leather
- https://polyhaven.com/license

The character design references, projected costume textures, fitted robe/collar/shoulder pieces, bow, quiver, rig adaptations, and rendering/integration code were made for this project. Original CC0 data has been modified; the listed authors do not endorse this character or the game.
